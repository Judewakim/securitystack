Find out what's exposed in your AWS account.

A free, read-only check for the 18 security gaps attackers and auditors look for first. It takes about two minutes, and nothing in your account changes.

How it stays safe

  • You create a role with AWS's own SecurityAudit policy. It can list and describe settings, but can't change anything or open your files and databases.
  • Only our scanner can use that role, and only with a one-time code made for your scan.
  • No passwords or access keys are ever entered here.
  • Results are deleted after 24 hours. Delete the role when you're done.

What it checks

Root account protection, MFA, old access keys, admin-level policies, public S3 buckets, open SSH/RDP and database ports, public or unencrypted databases, disk encryption, and whether CloudTrail, GuardDuty, Security Hub, Config, Inspector and Access Analyzer are on, in every region you use.

Scan your AWS account

  1. Create the read-only role

    Pick the region you use most, then open AWS. You'll land on a pre-filled CloudFormation page: tick the box acknowledging IAM resources and choose Create stack. It's ready in about a minute.
