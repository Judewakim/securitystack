Turn on AWS security in one click.

SecurityStack switches on the threat detection, audit logging and compliance checks AWS already offers, wires them together, and emails you when something serious happens. One CloudFormation stack, about ten minutes, and nothing you already run is touched.

Deploy SecurityStack

Opens a pre-filled CloudFormation page in your AWS account. Pick the region you use most; add other regions afterwards.

What happens when you deploy

  1. Each service is checked first. Anything you already run is left exactly as it is; only what's missing is turned on.
  2. Findings from every region flow into one place, and new critical or high-severity issues are emailed to you once.
  3. Every Monday you get a posture score and the top issues to fix, as an email and a report you can hand to an auditor or insurer.
  4. Deleting the stack removes only what SecurityStack added. Your logs are kept.

What it won't do

  • Change your existing resources. Two optional fixes (EBS encryption by default, account-wide S3 Block Public Access) stay off unless you turn them on.
  • Send your data anywhere. Everything runs in your account; nothing calls home.
  • Make you compliant on its own. It supports evidence for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls.

Cost: the AWS services bill your account directly, typically $20–45 a month for a small account, after 15–30 day free trials. See the breakdown. Extra regions: deploy the region stack in each one.
